- `Accounting::VatConsistencyQuery` (I7): declared balance (grid 71 − 72) vs ledger movement of 450100 − 410100 on posted lines of the period. Lines with no `vat_code` (State payments, manual entries) are reported as `ungridded_net` and excluded; `residual` must be 0. Invariant spec: `spec/invariants/i7_vat_return_matches_ledger_spec.rb`.
- Per-document check base × rate ≈ VAT (tolerance 0.05 €), listed not blocking.
- Panel shown on `vat_declarations/show`.
- Filed-period lock: `Accounting::Actions::ValidateInvoice` refuses posting an invoice dated inside a submitted/accepted declaration. **Gap:** manual journal entries are not guarded (a JournalEntry validation has too wide a blast radius).
- Deferred: grid → lines drill-down, regularizations (grids 61–64) sub-table, exports of the panel.
