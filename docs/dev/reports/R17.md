# R17 — Closing regularizations (v1)

- Model `accounting_accruals` (migration `20260929000500`): type (deferred charge / accrued income / accrued charge / deferred income), description, total, covered period, charge-or-income account, regularization account (default per type), optional source entry, generated entry, reversal entry. `accounting_invoice_lines` gains `service_start` / `service_end` (read by the cut-off lists).
- `Accounting::Accrual#amount_at(cut_off)`: deferral = total × unexpired days ÷ period days (leap years handled: 366-day period tested); accrual = total × elapsed days ÷ period days. Table-driven spec (annual insurance paid in October → 897.53 at 31 Dec).
- `Accounting::BookAccrual`: one balanced **draft** entry (misc journal, dated at the year end); `Accounting::ReverseAccrual`: the opposite **draft** on the first day of the next fiscal year (refused if that year does not exist yet).
- `Accounting::AccrualsReportQuery` + page `/accounting/accruals`: rows with amount, entry state and "missing reversal" flag; **I11** checks (Σ validated regularizations per type = balance of the type's account at the cut-off; spec `spec/invariants/i11_*`, the reference ledger carries a deferred insurance premium); the two cut-off lists (recorded ≤ 30 days after closing for a document dated before it, or dated after it for a service ended before it; year's invoices whose service runs past the closing).
- Deviations / open points: see `QUESTIONS.md` (PCMN 491/493 vs the chart's 4902xx/4922xx; accrual-side formula). Monthly reversal parameter (spec: next year *or* next month) not built: reversal is on the next fiscal year's first day.
- Deferred: service-period fields on the invoice line form (columns exist, no input yet — the cut-off list 2 stays empty until they are filled, e.g. by import or console), invoice-to-accrual prefill, closing-checklist warning for missing reversals, edit of an accrual, reversal of a validated reversal, exports.
