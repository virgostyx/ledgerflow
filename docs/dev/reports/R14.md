# R14 — Cash forecast (v1)

- `Accounting::CashForecastQuery`: weekly columns (13 weeks or 26 for the 6-month horizon) — opening · inflows · outflows · closing, alert row under a threshold. Opening = Σ ledger cash (55/57) to date. Open receivables/payables come from `Accounting::OpenLineSql.open_scope` (extracted from `AgedBalanceQuery`, so R04 and R14 share one reconstruction; inflows from receivables equal R04's customer total when everything falls in the horizon — tested).
- Overdue items go in week 1. Negative open amounts (credit notes, unallocated payments) follow the same rule.
- Scenarios: **base** = due date; **prudent** = customer receipts slip by the client's observed average delay (amount-weighted, from settled allocations, never negative) + N days (default 10); **optimistic** = receipts N days early (default 5). Payables and manual items never move. The spec did not define "optimistic": this is my reading.
- VAT: net 450100 − 410100 balance to date, paid on the next 20th (legal due day); a refund is never counted on.
- Manual and recurring items: one table `accounting_cash_forecast_items` (recurrence once/monthly/quarterly/yearly, direction, amount, end date) instead of the spec's two (`recurring_cash_items`, `cash_forecast_items`) which carry the same columns. Add/remove on the report page.
- Chart (`Charts::CashForecast`): closing balance per week, one line per scenario, threshold line.
- Deferred: exports, editing an item, the VAT date per filing frequency (the 20th is used for both monthly and quarterly filers), drill-down from a week to its documents, alert threshold saved per entity (it is a URL parameter).
