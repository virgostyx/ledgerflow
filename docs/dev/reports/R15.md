# R15 — Cash-flow statement (v1)

- `Accounting::CashFlowStatement` (both methods from one ledger read) + `Accounting::CashFlowCategories` (default category per PCMN code family). Cash = accounts 55/57. Closing entries are ignored; the opening (carry-forward) entry defines opening cash instead of counting as a flow.
- **I9** (`spec/invariants/i9_*`): indirect total = direct total = closing − opening cash, every fiscal year of the reference ledger. It follows from double entry: whole entries sum to zero over all accounts, so `result − Σ non-cash balance-sheet movements = Δcash` however the accounts are grouped.
- **Indirect:** net result + class 63 add-back ± stocks (3), trade receivables (40), trade payables (44), tax & social payables (45), other operating (the rest: 41, 46, 48, 49, 16, 29…; net of the non-cash part of the add-back) | investing = fixed assets (20–28, net of the 630 add-back offset) + cash investments (50–54) | financing = capital/reserves/grants (10–15) + borrowings and dividends (17, 42, 43, 47) | internal transfers (58).
- **Direct:** each cash movement is attributed line by line to the counterpart of the same entry (exact attribution; equals a pro rata split whenever counterparts share a sign — the spec's wording only covers that case). `accounts.cash_flow_category` (settings form) overrides the family default; anything with neither goes to "Non classé", never dropped. Changing a category moves flows between sections but never the net change (tested).
- Chart: waterfall opening → operating → investing → financing → closing (`Charts::CashFlowWaterfall`, direct-method totals). XLSX export with one sheet per method.
