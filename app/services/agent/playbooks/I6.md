## Symptom
Total assets differ from total liabilities in the annual accounts.
## Frequent causes
1. An account not attached to a rubric (check C13).
2. An unbalanced entry (check C01).
3. The result of the year not carried to the liabilities.
## Checks
- `get_financial_statements` (assets, liabilities): the difference and the warning on unmapped accounts.
- `get_consistency_findings` (checks C13 and C01).
## Interpretation
If an unmapped account has a balance equal to the difference, it is the cause.
## Fix
1. Map the account to its rubric. [[screen:accounts]]
## Prevention
Create accounts from the standard chart.
## Escalate
When no account explains the difference.
