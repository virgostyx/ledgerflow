title: A debit confirmed by hand is not on any statement

## Symptom
A payment batch whose debit was confirmed by hand, with no line of a bank statement tied to it.
## Frequent causes
1. The statement that holds the debit was not imported yet (the bank delivers it a day or two later).
2. The debit was confirmed on a wrong date or a wrong bank reference.
3. The payment was not debited at all.
## Checks
- `get_audit_trail` (the batch): who confirmed the debit, with which reason and bank reference.
## Interpretation
Nothing proves the money left the bank until a line of the statement is tied to the batch. A statement that arrived and was not recognised usually carries another reference or another date than the one that was given.
## Fix
1. Import the statement of the bank, then accept the proposal made for the batch. [[screen:bank_reconciliation]]
2. If the statement holds no such debit, ask the bank, and cancel the confirmation with the owners if the payment did not leave. [[screen:payments]]
## Prevention
Confirm a debit by hand only after seeing it in the application of the bank, and import the statements every day.
## Escalate
When the debit is still missing a week after its date: the supplier may be unpaid and the entries are on the books.
