## Symptom
For a VAT return, the figures differ from the VAT accounts in the ledger (451 less 411).
## Frequent causes
1. A VAT payment not lettered or wrongly allocated.
2. An entry without a VAT code.
3. A manual regularization.
4. A filed period modified afterwards.
## Checks
- `get_vat_return` (the period): the grids and the consistency panel.
- `get_ledger` (accounts 451 and 411): the movements of the period.
- `get_consistency_findings` (check C09).
## Interpretation
A residual equal to one entry's VAT points to a missing code or a manual entry. A residual after a filing points to a change in the period after filing.
## Fix
1. Correct the VAT code on the entry, or post a regularization in the open period. [[screen:journal]]
2. Check the return of the period. [[screen:vat]]
## Prevention
Lock the period when the return is filed. [[screen:periods]]
## Escalate
When the return was filed.
