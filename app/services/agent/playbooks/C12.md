title: Large gap between document date and accounting date

## Symptom
An invoice booked more than about three months after (or before) its document date.
## Frequent causes
1. A late invoice received after the period.
2. A wrong document date or a wrong entry date.
3. A prepayment or accrual booked on the invoice date by choice.
## Checks
- `get_journal_entry` (the invoice's entry): the two dates.
- `search_documents` (the invoice number): the date on the document.
## Interpretation
A late invoice is a real gap: the expense may belong to an earlier period. A typing error is visible on the document itself.
## Fix
1. If a date is wrong, reverse the entry and post it with the right date. [[screen:journal]]
2. If the expense belongs to a closed period, ask whether a regularization is needed. [[screen:accruals]]
## Prevention
Book invoices when they arrive and keep the document date as written.
## Escalate
When the invoice belongs to a closed fiscal year or to a filed VAT period.
