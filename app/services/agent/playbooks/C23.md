title: The integrity of the books is compromised

## Symptom
The check of the integrity found that an entry no longer gives its fingerprint, that an anchor no longer follows from the entries it binds, or that the audit chain does not hold.
## Frequent causes
1. An entry, a line or the order of validation was changed in the database without going through the application (a restore, a script, a manual correction).
2. A backup of an older state was restored over the books after fingerprints and anchors were made.
3. A fault of the application between the validation of an entry and its fingerprint (rare: both are written in the same transaction).
## Checks
- `get_integrity_status`: the last anchor, the last check and what it found.
- `get_audit_trail` for the entry named by the anomaly: who touched it and when; an entry changed with no audit event was changed outside the application.
## Interpretation
Until it is explained, no certificate of integrity is issued, and the books cannot be said to be unchanged since they were anchored. Do not correct the entry by hand: its fingerprint and the anchors that cover it would not agree either way.
## Fix
1. Find which side moved: the entry or the fingerprint recorded in its anchor. The audit trail names what went through the application. [[screen:consistency]]
2. Keep everything as it is and ask the owners to decide, with the person who has access to the database, whether to restore the books from a backup made before the change. [[screen:consistency]]
## Prevention
Never edit entries, their lines, their order or their instants in the database directly; restore only whole backups, and say so in the audit trail.
## Escalate
Always: the proof that the books were not altered is lost until the cause is known.
