title: Gap in journal numbering
validated: false

## Symptom
Numbers are missing in the sequence of a journal for an open fiscal year.
## Frequent causes
1. A draft that had received a number was deleted.
2. An entry was removed outside the application.
3. An import was cancelled half way.
## Checks
- `get_audit_trail` (journal entries, around the missing numbers): deletions and cancellations.
- `get_ledger` (journal, period): the entries on each side of the gap.
## Interpretation
A deletion in the audit trail explains the gap. No trace of any event is serious: a number without a trace means the audit trail or the data were changed outside the application.
## Fix
1. If the gap is explained by the audit trail, acknowledge the anomaly with the explanation. [[screen:consistency]]
2. If it is not, do not renumber: ask the accountant and leave the anomaly open. [[screen:consistency]]
## Prevention
Number entries only at posting, never at drafting, and never delete a posted entry: reverse it. [[screen:journal]]
## Escalate
When no event explains the gap: a missing number in the legal sequence must be justified.
