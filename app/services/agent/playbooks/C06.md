## Symptom
Validated lines on an account that was archived (made inactive).
## Frequent causes
1. The account was archived after lines were posted on it.
2. A recurring entry or an import still points to the archived account.
## Checks
- `search_accounts` (the account code, include archived): its status.
- `get_ledger` (the account): the lines and their dates.
## Interpretation
Lines older than the archive date are normal history; new lines after it come from a recurring entry or an import mapping.
## Fix
1. Reactivate the account if it is still needed, or move the lines to the right account by reversal. [[screen:accounts]]
2. Change the recurring entry or the import mapping that still uses it. [[screen:imports]]
## Prevention
Archive an account only when its balance is zero and nothing recurring uses it.
## Escalate
When the balance is not zero.
