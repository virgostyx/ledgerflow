## Symptom
A domestic customer above the threshold of the annual customer listing whose VAT number is missing or invalid.
## Frequent causes
1. The number was never entered.
2. The number has a typing error (check digits).
3. The customer is not subject to VAT and needs a different treatment.
## Checks
- `search_partners` (the partner): the number as recorded and its validity.
- `get_ledger` (the customer account, the partner): the turnover behind the amount.
## Interpretation
An empty number is an omission; a number that fails the check digits is a typing error. A private person is not listed.
## Fix
1. Enter or correct the number on the partner record. [[screen:partners]]
## Prevention
Require the number when creating a business customer.
## Escalate
When the customer says it has no number but trades as a business.
