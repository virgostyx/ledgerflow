## Symptom
An entry whose date falls outside the dates of the fiscal year it belongs to.
## Frequent causes
1. The entry date was typed with the wrong year.
2. The fiscal year dates were changed after entries were posted.
3. An import assigned the wrong fiscal year.
## Checks
- `get_journal_entry` (id of the entry): date and fiscal year.
- `get_company_context`: the dates of the fiscal years.
## Interpretation
If the date is one year off, it is a typing error. If many entries are affected, a fiscal year was changed or an import is at fault.
## Fix
1. Reverse the entry and enter it again with the right date, in an open period. [[screen:journal]]
2. Check the dates of the fiscal years. [[screen:fiscal_years]]
## Prevention
Lock closed periods so that nobody can post into them. [[screen:periods]]
## Escalate
When the entry is in a closed or locked period, or when the fiscal year dates were changed.
