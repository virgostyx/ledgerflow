title: Total debit equals total credit
validated: false

## Symptom
For a fiscal year, the sum of all validated debits differs from the sum of all credits.
## Frequent causes
1. An unbalanced entry (check C01).
2. A line written outside the application.
## Checks
- `get_consistency_findings` (check C01): unbalanced entries.
- `get_trial_balance`: the totals of the year.
## Interpretation
If C01 lists an entry whose difference equals this one, it is the cause. Otherwise the data were changed outside the application.
## Fix
1. Correct the unbalanced entry by reversal. [[screen:journal]]
## Prevention
No write outside the application.
## Escalate
Always.
