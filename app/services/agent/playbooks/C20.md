id: C20
title: The history does not reproduce a frozen snapshot
validated: false
---
## Symptom
A snapshot that was frozen (the closing of a year, a VAT return that was filed) differs from what the history of the books gives at the instant it was taken.
## Frequent causes
1. The snapshot was changed after it was taken (a database edit, an import).
2. An entry, a lettering or a name was changed in the database without going through the application, so the history no longer says what was true.
3. The clock of the application server and the one of the database are not the same: an entry validated just around the snapshot falls on the other side.
## Checks
- `get_audit_trail` for the closing run or the VAT return: who touched it and when.
- `get_ledger` (an account of the snapshot): the validation instant of the entries around it.
## Interpretation
Until it is explained, the figures of the frozen snapshot and the historical views around that instant cannot be relied on. Do not correct either side by hand.
## Fix
1. Find out which side moved: the audit trail names the actions made through the application; a change that has no audit row was made outside it. [[screen:consistency]]
2. Keep the snapshot as it is, and ask the owners to decide with the person who has access to the database. [[screen:consistency]]
## Prevention
Keep the clocks of the servers synchronised, and never edit a snapshot, an entry or a lettering in the database directly.
## Escalate
Always: a snapshot that the history does not reproduce puts the figures filed with the closing or the tax return in doubt.
