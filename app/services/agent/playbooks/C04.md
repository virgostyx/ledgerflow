## Symptom
A customer (40), supplier (44) or bank (55) account with a balance on the wrong side: a credit balance on a customer account, a debit balance on a supplier account, a credit on a bank account.
## Frequent causes
1. Payments or advances received that are not lettered against an invoice.
2. A credit note not applied to its invoice.
3. A posting error between two partners.
## Checks
- `get_aged_balance` (kind customer or supplier, as of the date): the column of what is not allocated, by partner.
- `list_unreconciled` (the partner): open lines that could be lettered together.
- `get_ledger` (the account, the latest movements): the last entries on the account.
## Interpretation
If one partner carries the whole inverted balance and has an open payment line with no invoice to match, it is an advance or a payment to letter. If the amount equals a credit note that is not applied, apply it. If the balance is spread over several partners, look for a posting error.
## Fix
1. Letter the payment with the invoice it settles. [[screen:lettering]]
2. If the balance is a justified advance, say so and acknowledge the anomaly with that reason. [[screen:consistency]]
3. If it is a posting error, reverse the entry and post it on the right partner. [[screen:journal]]
## Prevention
Letter payments when they are booked; use the lettering suggestions after each bank import. [[screen:lettering]]
## Escalate
When the amount is large against the partner's turnover, or when it does not match any open line.
