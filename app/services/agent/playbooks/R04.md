## Symptom
The total of the aged balance of customers or suppliers differs from the balance of the control account (40 or 44) in the trial balance.
## Frequent causes
1. Entries on the control account without a partner.
2. A different date of the two reports.
3. Lines on the control account posted outside the invoice flow.
## Checks
- `get_aged_balance` (kind, as of): the total by partner and the lines without partner.
- `get_trial_balance` (the control account, same date): the balance.
- `get_ledger` (the control account, lines without a partner).
## Interpretation
A difference equal to lines without a partner is established. Different dates explain a difference that disappears with the same date.
## Fix
1. Give the lines their partner. [[screen:journal]]
## Prevention
Require a partner on every line of a control account.
## Escalate
When the difference does not match any line.
