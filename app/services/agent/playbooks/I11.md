title: Regularizations agree with accounts 490 to 493
validated: false
---
## Symptom
The regularizations booked do not match the balances of accounts 490 to 493.
## Frequent causes
1. A regularization posted by hand on those accounts.
2. A reversal posted without its regularization.
## Checks
- `get_ledger` (accounts 490 to 493): the movements.
- `get_trial_balance` (the accounts): the balances.
## Interpretation
A difference equal to one entry points to a manual posting that the regularization screen does not know.
## Fix
1. Rebook the movement through the regularization screen. [[screen:accruals]]
## Prevention
Post regularizations only through their screen.
## Escalate
When the year is closed.
