## Symptom
An account with a balance that no rubric of the balance sheet or income statement picks up: its amount is missing from the annual accounts.
## Frequent causes
1. An account was created with a code that the mapping does not cover.
2. The mapping of an account was removed.
## Checks
- `search_accounts` (the code): its class and type.
- `get_financial_statements`: the warning about unmapped accounts, and the balance sheet difference.
## Interpretation
The amount of the account is the amount missing from the statements. If the balance sheet does not balance by exactly that amount, the cause is established.
## Fix
1. Map the account to its rubric, or use an account of the standard chart. [[screen:accounts]]
## Prevention
Create accounts from the standard chart, which carries the mapping.
## Escalate
When the account holds a large balance whose nature is unclear.
