## Symptom
The balance of the bank statement does not match the balance of the bank account in the books at the same date.
## Frequent causes
1. A line of the statement that is not booked.
2. An entry dated after the statement date.
3. A break in the chain of statement balances (a statement missing).
4. A duplicate.
## Checks
- `get_bank_reconciliation` (the bank account, the date): the lines of the statement not in the books and the book lines not on the statement.
- `get_ledger` (the bank account around the date): the entries near the date.
## Interpretation
One unmatched statement line equal to the difference is the cause. A date after the cut-off explains a difference that disappears a few days later. A difference that equals a whole statement points to a missing import.
## Fix
1. Book the unmatched line from the bank statement. [[screen:bank]]
2. Import the missing statement. [[screen:imports]]
## Prevention
Import statements regularly and reconcile each one when imported. [[screen:bank]]
## Escalate
When the difference does not match any line.
