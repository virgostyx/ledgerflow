title: Lettered group with a non-zero total
validated: false
---
## Symptom
A set of lines lettered together whose debits and credits do not add up to zero.
## Frequent causes
1. A line was changed after lettering.
2. A partial lettering was recorded as total.
3. A write-off was not generated.
## Checks
- `get_ledger` (the account, filter lettered): the lines of the group and their amounts.
- `get_audit_trail` (the lettering): when it was made and by whom.
## Interpretation
A non-zero total equal to a small amount is an unrecorded difference (write-off). A larger total means a line is in the group by mistake.
## Fix
1. Remove the lettering and make it again with the right lines. [[screen:lettering]]
2. For a small difference, letter with a write-off. [[screen:lettering]]
## Prevention
Letter only through the lettering screen, which checks the total.
## Escalate
When the difference is not small or the lettering is old.
