## Symptom
An entry whose total debit differs from its total credit. The database should refuse it; this check catches what slipped past.
## Frequent causes
1. A line was deleted or edited directly in the database, outside the application.
2. An import or a migration wrote lines without the balancing line.
3. A rounding difference on a split line.
## Checks
- `get_journal_entry` (id of the entry): lists the lines, the totals and the difference.
- `get_audit_trail` (the entry): who touched it and when.
## Interpretation
A difference of a few cents on a many-line entry points to rounding. A difference equal to one line's amount points to a missing or deleted line. The audit trail showing a change after posting is a sign of a direct edit.
## Fix
1. Open the entry and compare its lines with the source document. [[screen:journal]]
2. Do not edit a validated entry: reverse it and enter the correct one. [[screen:journal]]
## Prevention
Never write to the ledger tables outside the application; imports go through the import screen. [[screen:imports]]
## Escalate
Always: a blocking anomaly on a posted entry means the books were changed outside the rules. Ask the accountant before correcting.
