title: Base times rate inconsistent with the VAT

## Symptom
An invoice whose VAT differs from the sum of line base times rate by more than five cents.
## Frequent causes
1. The VAT amount was typed by hand instead of calculated.
2. A line has the wrong VAT rate.
3. A rounding made on the total instead of per line.
## Checks
- `get_journal_entry` (the invoice's entry): VAT lines against the base lines.
- `get_vat_return` (the period): how the invoice enters the grids.
## Interpretation
A difference of a few cents above the tolerance is rounding. A difference of a whole rate step (for example 21 instead of 6) is a wrong rate on a line.
## Fix
1. Correct the invoice if it is a draft; otherwise reverse it and post it again with the right rate. [[screen:journal]]
2. If the VAT return of the period was filed, correct it in the next return. [[screen:vat]]
## Prevention
Let the application calculate the VAT; do not edit the VAT amount by hand.
## Escalate
When the period's VAT return is filed.
