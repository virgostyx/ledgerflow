id: C21
title: A balance does not move as the validated lines say
validated: false
---
## Symptom
In the report of changes, the variation of an account between two instants is not the sum of the lines validated between them (the closing shows a gap).
## Frequent causes
1. A validated entry, its place or its instant was changed in the database without going through the application.
2. A result kept for a past instant (the cache of the historical views) no longer matches the books.
3. A line was moved to another account, or an account was merged, outside the application.
## Checks
- `get_trial_balance` at both instants (`known_at`), then `get_ledger` for the account named by the anomaly: the lines validated between the two.
- `get_audit_trail` for the account and its entries.
## Interpretation
Until the gap is explained, the reports read at past instants and the report of changes cannot be relied on for that account. Do not correct the figures by hand.
## Fix
1. Compare the account at the two instants with its lines, and find the line or the entry that explains the gap. [[screen:consistency]]
2. If the books were changed outside the application, ask the owners to decide, with the person who has access to the database, how to restore them. [[screen:consistency]]
## Prevention
Never edit entries, their order or their instants in the database directly.
## Escalate
Always: the history of the books is in doubt.
