title: Fixed-asset register differs from the ledger

## Symptom
The register of fixed assets does not match the asset accounts, the accumulated depreciation or the year's charge in the ledger.
## Frequent causes
1. An asset bought but not entered in the register.
2. A depreciation entry posted by hand that the register does not know.
3. A disposal posted in one place only.
## Checks
- `get_trial_balance` (accounts 21 to 28 and their depreciation): the ledger side.
- `get_ledger` (the asset account): movements without a register counterpart.
## Interpretation
A difference equal to one asset's cost points to a missing register entry; a difference equal to one year's depreciation points to a manual or missing depreciation entry.
## Fix
1. Add the missing asset to the register, or post the depreciation through the register. [[screen:fixed_assets]]
## Prevention
Post all depreciation through the register, never by hand.
## Escalate
When a disposal or an impairment is involved.
