title: Regularization without a reversal

## Symptom
A regularization (prepayment or accrual) that is booked but has no reversal generated.
## Frequent causes
1. The reversal was never generated after the year opened.
2. The fiscal year following it does not exist yet.
## Checks
- `get_ledger` (accounts 490 to 493): the regularization and any reversal.
- `get_company_context`: whether the next fiscal year exists.
## Interpretation
If the next year exists and no reversal is there, it was forgotten. If it does not exist, the reversal cannot be made yet.
## Fix
1. Generate the reversal from the regularization. [[screen:accruals]]
## Prevention
Generate the reversals right after opening the new fiscal year.
## Escalate
When the amount is large or the year is closed.
